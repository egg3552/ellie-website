GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

curiosity?

What sort of things will I find here?+

Hand-painted stones and other small arts and crafts. The collection will grow as new ideas take shape, so there will be more little wonders to discover along the way.

Can I buy through the website?+

For now, this is a place to explore the style and discover what’s being made. There’s no online checkout. More information will be shared as the business gets ready to launch.

When can I see the collection?+

Photographs of the handmade pieces will be added soon. Pop back for a closer look at the collection as it takes shape.

Will every piece look exactly the same?+

No two natural stones are quite alike. Different shapes and little variations in brushstrokes are part of what makes handmade pieces special.
